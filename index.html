Projects

FlashBack: 270° Panoramic Photo Booth

16 frames → one 270° panorama, printed on the spot

A battery-powered booth that rotates on a servo turntable, stitches a panorama in OpenCV, and prints it as a physical keepsake. I redesigned the load path onto a ball bearing and built the stitching pipeline and touchscreen UI.

  • SolidWorks
  • Python
  • OpenCV
  • Raspberry Pi

Smart Nightstand Hub

$24.79 in parts · working fit in 2 print iterations

A bedside display for weather, calendar, and live bus times, built to cut my phone dependence. Adhesive-free press-fit enclosure designed around the boards inside it, with the temperature sensor thermally isolated from the electronics.

  • SolidWorks
  • FDM 3D printing
  • DFM
  • ESP32

Fold or Roll: Networked Dice Game

Two computers synced at ~1 Hz over an IoT REST API

A two-player MATLAB game kept in sync across separate machines using ThingSpeak as a shared database. I designed the networking, the turn gating, and a compressed data format that keeps each player's dice private until showdown.

  • MATLAB
  • App Designer
  • REST APIs
  • JSON

About

Hello

I'm a second-year mechanical engineering student at UC Davis. What I keep gravitating toward is the seam between disciplines — the enclosure that has to fit the board, the servo settle time that decides whether an image stitches, the sensor placement that determines whether a reading means anything.

That's shaped how I work. On a 22-person team I ended up as the person translating between the mechanical and EECS subteams, because I could tell whether a failure was mechanical, electrical, or software before we started pulling things apart. At Cogwell I spent two summers on the measurement side of that same instinct: calibrating precision instruments, tracing PCB faults from schematics, and writing the automated procedures that verify them.

I use AI coding tools — mainly Claude Code — to move faster on firmware and prototyping. I treat that as something I direct rather than something that does the work: I wire and test every subsystem on real hardware, and I can walk through what the code does.

I'm looking for a Summer 2027 mechanical engineering internship in the Bay Area, ideally somewhere mechanical design meets electronics — robotics, automation, consumer hardware, or test engineering. The fastest way to reach me is email.
