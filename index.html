About

Hello,

I am a second-year mechanical engineering student at UC Davis. What I strive to do is connect the mechanical side with the electrical side. Doing this lets me understand how entire projects work, not just the part I was assigned to. This allows me to produce the best possible solutions for my problems I am faced with.

This has shaped how I work. On a 22-person team I contributed to both the mechanical and EECS (electrical engineering & computer science) subteams. Because of my experience, I could better tell whether a failure was mechanical, electrical, or software-related before we started pulling things apart. At Cogwell, I spent two summers gaining a true appreciation for precision equipment. I calibrated these instruments with procedures and standards, traced PCB faults from schematics, and created custom scripts to verify these repairs.

I use AI coding tools like Claude Code to move faster on firmware and prototyping. I treat it as something I direct rather than something that does the work: I wire and test every subsystem on real hardware.

I'm looking for a Summer 2027 mechanical engineering internship in the Bay Area, ideally somewhere mechanical design meets electronics, robotics, automation, consumer hardware, or test engineering.
